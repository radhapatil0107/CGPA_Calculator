💡 How are SGPA and CGPA Calculated?

SGPA Formula:

SGPA = Σ(Subject Credits × Grade Points) / Σ(Subject Credits)

Each grade letter is mapped to points (e.g. O = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8, B+ = 7, etc.).

CGPA Formula:

CGPA = Σ(Semester SGPA × Semester Credits) / Σ(Semester Credits)

CGPA is the credit-weighted average across all completed semesters in the degree.
